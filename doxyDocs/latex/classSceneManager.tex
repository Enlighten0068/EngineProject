\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Manages a stack of scenes. 

The \doxylink{classSceneManager}{Scene\+Manager} handles scene transitions by maintaining a stack of scenes. Only the top scene is active (receives Update and Render calls).

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Supports pushing, popping, and setting scenes. 
\end{DoxyNote}

\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Demo 2D \doxylink{classScene}{Scene} with a dynamic camera that follows the player. 

This scene features a camera that follows the player entity, making it suitable for larger levels where the player can move beyond the screen. It includes platform collision and player movement mechanics.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
This scene does not include enemies (unlike \doxylink{classDemo2DFixedScene}{Demo2\+DFixed\+Scene}). 
\end{DoxyNote}

\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Controller for the player entity, handling movement, jumping, and physics. 

This class processes keyboard and gamepad input, updates the player position and velocity, and handles jump physics (variable jump height, gravity, and grounded detection). It also integrates with the audio system to play jump and landing sound effects.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The controller works in conjunction with the physics system and collision resolution performed in the scene. 
\end{DoxyNote}

Updates the player\textquotesingle{}s state (movement, jump, physics). 

Should be called once per frame. Reads input, updates velocity and position, and handles jump logic. Also detects landing events and plays sound effects.

This method handles horizontal movement, jump detection and its physics, vertical velocity, landing detection, boundary clamping and death.

\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Defines the position, rotation, and scale of an entity. 

This is the most fundamental component for any entity that exists in the world. It defines where the entity is located, how it is oriented, and its size.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Rotation is stored in radians for mathematical consistency. 
\end{DoxyNote}

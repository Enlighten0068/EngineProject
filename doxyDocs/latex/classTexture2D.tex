\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
2D texture wrapper for Open\+GL textures. 

Loads images from files (using stb\+\_\+image) and manages the Open\+GL texture object. Supports wrapping modes (GL\+\_\+\+REPEAT) and mipmap generation.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Textures are loaded with vertical flip enabled (stbi\+\_\+set\+\_\+flip\+\_\+vertically\+\_\+on\+\_\+load). 
\end{DoxyNote}

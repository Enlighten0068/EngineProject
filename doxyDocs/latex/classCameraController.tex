\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Controls a 2D camera with follow and zoom capabilities. 

The \doxylink{classCameraController}{Camera\+Controller} manages camera movement in two modes:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Follow mode: smoothly tracks a target entity (player)
\item Manual mode: controlled by WASD keys It also handles zoom via mouse scroll and clamps the camera to world boundaries.
\end{DoxyItemize}

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The camera position is smoothly interpolated (lerp) when in follow mode. 
\end{DoxyNote}

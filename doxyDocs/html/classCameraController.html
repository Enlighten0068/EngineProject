Detailed Description

Controls a 2D camera with follow and zoom capabilities.

The CameraController manages camera movement in two modes:

  • Follow mode: smoothly tracks a target entity (player)
  • Manual mode: controlled by WASD keys It also handles zoom via mouse scroll and clamps the camera to world boundaries.
Note
The camera position is smoothly interpolated (lerp) when in follow mode.
